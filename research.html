RESEARCH

EcoHealth

Connecting nature, biodiversity, and human health.

My research interests lie at the intersection of environmental health and ecology. I am particularly interested in understanding how natural environments, biodiversity, and environmental change influence human health and well-being.

01

Nature & Human Health

How do greenness, access to natural environments, and everyday ecological experiences shape mental and physical health?

02

Biodiversity & Health

Can biodiversity and ecological quality explain health benefits that conventional measures of green space do not capture?

03

Ecological Exposure & Health Equity

Who is exposed to healthier ecological environments, and how are environmental benefits and risks distributed across people and places?

I founded **Ecomoneyna** to create a space where environmental interest could become practical ecological action. As founder and president, I have helped shape the organization’s direction while leading field-based activities, environmental education, and community projects.

The work has included ecological field surveys, urban swallow-related activities, and initiatives that encourage people to engage more directly with local environments. I want Ecomoneyna to remain a platform where research, practice, and public communication can meet.

As the organization continues, my role is to set the broader direction, represent the group publicly, and help turn ideas into projects that people can participate in.

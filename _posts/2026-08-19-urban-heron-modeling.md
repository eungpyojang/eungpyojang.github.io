I modeled habitat suitability for **five Ardeidae species** using MaxEnt and spatial environmental variables.

The project focused on identifying ecological conditions associated with potential urban habitat and understanding how different species respond to landscape structure, vegetation, water, elevation, and human-modified environments.

This work strengthened my interest in combining species-distribution modeling with questions about urban coexistence and, more broadly, how ecological conditions can be translated into human and environmental health research.

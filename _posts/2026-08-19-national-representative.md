I participated in the **815 Presidential People’s Appointment Ceremony** as one of 80 nationally selected citizen representatives.

During the program, I shared perspectives connected to ecological data, citizen science, and environmental participation, and took part in public-facing communication through interviews.

This experience reinforced my interest in communicating environmental issues beyond academic settings. I hope to keep contributing to conversations where research, ecological practice, and public understanding intersect.
